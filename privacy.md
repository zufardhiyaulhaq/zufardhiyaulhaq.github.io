  <p class="eyebrow">Privacy</p>
  <h1 class="page-title">What this site collects</h1>
  <p class="page-lead">This site does not track you. No analytics, no ads, and no cookies set by the site.</p>
</div>

<div class="prose">
  <h2>The site itself</h2>
  <p>It is static HTML. There are no analytics, no tracking scripts, no forms, no cookies, and no local storage.</p>

  <h2>Services your browser talks to</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>GitHub Pages</strong> hosts the site. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security, as described in <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection">GitHub's docs</a> and the <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement">GitHub Privacy Statement</a>.</li>
    <li><strong>Google Fonts</strong> serves the fonts on every page, so your browser sends Google its IP address when the fonts load. See the <a href="https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy">Google Fonts privacy FAQ</a>.</li>
    <li><strong>jsDelivr</strong> serves the diagram library, only on posts that contain diagrams.</li>
    <li>A few older posts embed content from other sites: GitHub Gists, one YouTube video, and one image from gimx.fr. These load from their own servers, and YouTube may set its own cookies when the video loads.</li>
  </ul>
